### Description

Sentient, Humanoid Lifeforms made from Bio Energy.
All Faunoids has at least some Magia Eneri at birth.


### Types

#### Basic Faunoid

#Faunoids/Basic_Faunoid 

There's not much to these Faunoids. However, they have just as much potential to become a bright light in the darkness.


#### Beasts

#Faunoids/Beasts 

Faunoids with Fauna attributes. Such as having ears, tails, etc., depending on the type.

##### Wolf Faunoid

Their heightened senses & incredible speed barely leave "prey" have a moment to breathe during hunts.

##### Fox Faunoid

Unlike Wolves, they like to have some fun during a hunt.
Some are Wise, some are Cunning.

##### Lizard Faunoid

Agile and stealthy.
Climbs on walls, has long tongues, and can mask with the environment.


##### Dragon Faunoid

An ancient variant, not much is currently known about them.



#### Aquarians

#Faunoids/Aquarians 

Excels in the water, not so much on land.

##### Fish Aquarian

Watch out! They may be at the bottom of the chain, however they still pack a punch.


#### Avianoids

#Faunoids/Avianoids

Excels in the sky, not so much in water.
