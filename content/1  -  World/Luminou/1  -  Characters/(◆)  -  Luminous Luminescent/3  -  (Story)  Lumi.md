### Origins

Many many years ago, even Lumi herself doesn't know where she was born from. She was raised by a Granny Tauri, who raised many others.

Occasionally she spent time with Lux Solaris during childhood, eventually becoming protectors of the light.


### Luminou and Lumen Knights' Origin

After a successful triumph over a Starcrusher type Nox Unda, discussions must be made for the future.
Lux gives the offer of leading the region and the LuKni to Lumi, believing that she would do better than them.

Lumi never truly knew why the offer was given to her, until Lux disappeared and never returned.


### Current

Part of why Luminou is mostly a friendly place, is because of Lumi. Locals follow her cheerful and supportive personality.
