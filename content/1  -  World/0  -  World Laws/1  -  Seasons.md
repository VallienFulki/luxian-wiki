### Solar Charge

|        |                    |
| ------ | ------------------ |
| Spring | "Almost Charged"   |
| Summer | "Full Charge"      |
| Autumn | "Degrading Charge" |
| Winter | "Recharging"       |
Solar Charge is gained from the Sun, and rests with the Moon.

Think of a battery. You charge it, but its charge degrades over time from use.
