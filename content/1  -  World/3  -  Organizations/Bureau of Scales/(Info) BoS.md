### What is BoS?

The Bureau of Scales is Luxian's Law Enforcement, that specializes in criminal cases such as murder or treason.

The Nox Unda is not the only threat that lurks in the shadows of Luxian.


### Judgement System

The Entity of Scales [[(Info)  Verity|Verity]] is responsible for weighing the scales of the accused, and bring judgement upon them if necessary.

No one is exempt from the scales. Not even the Region Leaders, nor the Entity themself.

When a Region Leader has committed a crime against their own region and/or other regions, it counts as treason and may likely be executed.


### Enforcement System

Enforcement is responsible for investigating potential crimes, but they cannot punish the accused.

When joining Enforcement, BoS will place a pact spell on the new officer. If this pact to never abuse power is broken, they fall into a coma until [[(Info)  Verity|Verity]] wakes them up.
Of course it's not a 100% foolproof spell.

Enforcement Officers are also required to take an exam every year to test their strength and mental status.

The [[(Info) LuKni|Lumen Knights]] and [[(Info) Nox Hunters|Nox Hunters]] are permitted to assist enforcement, but cannot take over cases.
