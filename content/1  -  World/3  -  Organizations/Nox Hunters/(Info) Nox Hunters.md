Nox Hunters are the Bounty Hunters of Luxian. Helping both [[(Info) LuKni|Lumen Knights]] and [[(Info) BoS|Bureau of Scales]] hunt Nox Unda and Wanted Criminals when they don't have enough resources.

They are often the kind that loves the thrill of danger by working alone. Sometimes they do work in teams, but it's not as common.
