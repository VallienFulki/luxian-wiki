# Land

From gothic-like architecture to the starry sea.
Has a few cities, and a few villages. 



# Culture

## General

Luminou's focus is the joy of creativity. Drawing, Cooking, Writing, are a few of many forms of creativity.
The other is festivity, living their lives to the fullest. Brightening the cities with festivals.


## Life & Death

Citizens of Luminou often believe in living their lives to the fullest, not letting negativity fully block their path.

When it's time for a being to pass, their life is honored during the Lantern Festival.
The Lantern Festival is believed to help the dead rest more peacefully, when they witness that life will always flourish.
While they're guided towards the afterlife using floating lanterns.
