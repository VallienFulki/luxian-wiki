### Backstory

#### Beginnings

Vivian had a happy childhood, free from duty. Or so, that was the plan of her sibling Eirian Nokoribi.

At the age of 18, Vivian had to put her corrupted sibling to rest. This is the beginning of Vivian's turn to inherit the Nokoribi's legacy.

She became deeply focused with fulfilling her duty as a Nokoribi, to the point where Zalika eventually came up with a plan to break these chains. Perhaps a plan, that will forever change Vivian's life.

#### Scintilla Rosa

The Captain of Nexus Flare: Zalika assigns him and Vivian to become work partners. At first she didn't really see the point, since she works well on her own.

#### Dawnweaver

Years later, Vivian & Rozan decide to take a step forward with both their relationship, & the future.

In order to "break free" from old legacies, they create a new legacy. They both agreed that sticking to their old family legacies will benefit no one anymore.

They marry as Dawnweavers, Weaving the Dawn towards a brighter tomorrow.

# Design

### Design Notes

#### Hair

Her flame-like ponytail & flicks can be de-summoned, due to a spell she used to "make" them.

#### Flames

The type of flame is important to note, as it depends on the version of her.

Flames of the Dawnweaver (Dawn Flame), contains colors of the Dawn Sky.


#### Fire Scliser V1

Vivian's sword has flame wings that appear while she holds the sword.

It disappears when she either stops holding it, or when someone else holds it.


#### Fire Scliser V2

Vivian's sword has flame wings that appear while she holds the sword.

It disappears when she either stops holding it, or when someone else holds it.

Also, **this version of Fire Scliser works differently than V1.**

Instead of a metal blade, it's a summonable fiery blade.

& It has different modes:

**Spellblade Mode**

AKA the sword mode.

**Spelltome Mode**

Bladeless, casts spells at a lower ME cost.

**Staff Mode**

Like a Spelltome, but as a staff just to look cool while casting spells.
