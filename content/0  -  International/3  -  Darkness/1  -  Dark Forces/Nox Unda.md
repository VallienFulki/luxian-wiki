## Overview

One of the most frequent and potent forms of Darkness in Luxian.
Corrupting those who sunk into the deep dark, to continue the cycle.


## Forms

|                        |                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Full Corruption        | Severe Corruption, the point of no return.<br>Victim becomes a hollow vessel for the Nox Unda to control. |
| Half Corruption        | Victim has permanent changes/injuries to their body, but still has sentient control of their body.        |
| Partial Corruption<br> | Non Severe Corruption, easy to treat.                                                                     |
