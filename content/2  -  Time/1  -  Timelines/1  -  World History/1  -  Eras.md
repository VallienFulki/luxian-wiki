### Scattered Light Era (50,000 Years Ago)

The "Old World" of Luxian that was ruled by Royalty.
At the end of the era, one Noble destroyed all Royal bloodlines due to their greedy thirst for power. Terms such as (Royal, Noble, King, Queen, etc.) are exclusive to fictional stories in the current era, due to the same Noble "resetting" the world.

The Divus population is more frequent in this era, which is the species Nobles are.

### Harmonic Light Era (5,000 Years Ago - Present)

The "Current World" of Luxian, ruled by more competent Region Leaders.
There's barely anyone that is aware of the previous era of Luxian.

Entities are created by the Entity of Luxian in this era, in hopes to keep the world in more of a balance.
