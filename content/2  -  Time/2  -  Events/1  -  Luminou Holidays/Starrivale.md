Once a year, Lumens (Luminou Locals) use Star Crystals to make fireworks. These are often used for festivals, as a way to celebrate happy bright moments.

In Luminou, it's considered to be respectful towards the fallen by simply enjoying life.
By enjoying life it's said to help the fallen rest more peacefully, since life is shown to continue flourishing.
