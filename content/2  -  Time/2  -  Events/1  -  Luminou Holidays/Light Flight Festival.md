|          |                                    |
| -------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Known As | Lantern Festival                   |
| Duration | Yearly - Mar. 1 - 3                |
| Location | Edge of Luminou, towards the ocean |

Once a year, Lumens (Luminou Locals) use Star Crystals to make flying lanterns. Fly a lantern to wish for someone's happiness, mourn for someone's passing, or a chance to guide those who are lost.

No matter why someone may fly a lantern, it's important to be respectful.
